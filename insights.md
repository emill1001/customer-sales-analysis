# Business Insights

## Customer Spending

High-value customers generated a significant proportion of total revenue, indicating opportunities for targeted loyalty campaigns.

---

## Product Popularity

A small number of menu items accounted for most purchases, suggesting strong customer preferences.

---

## Loyalty Program

Customers increased their engagement after joining the membership program, demonstrating the potential value of loyalty incentives.

---

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how SQL can be used to analyze customer purchasing behaviour, evaluate loyalty programs, and support business decision-making.
